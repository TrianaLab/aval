`aval stats --registry <repo> [--test <id>] [--since <duration|date>] [--by test|repository] [--output text|json]` reads the run records in the registry and aggregates them on demand: runs, hit rate, misses by cause, verification failures, shadow false hits, and total and saved test time, per test (or per source repository with `--by repository`) and in total. It writes nothing. Records that are unreadable, oversized, malformed or inconsistent are skipped and counted; reading stops after 1000 records or 64 pages per listing, with a warning. New format, provisional: `aval.trianalab.io/stats-report/v1` (`pkg/evidence` adds `StatsReport`, `TestStats`, `RepositoryStats`, `RunStats`, `MissCauses`, `StatsReportVersion` and `StatsUnknownRepository`). Run records gain the optional `verifiedResult` and `repository` (`--repository` on `aval run` and `aval record`; records without it count as `unknown`), and `evidence.ParseRunRecord` now rejects inconsistent records (`reused: true` requires outcome `verified` and `testDurationMs` 0; `verifiedResult` requires outcome `verified`), so `aval record` refuses a hand-edited record that claims a reuse that never happened. Docs explain pruning old records with ORAS.
