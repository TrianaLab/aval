`aval stats --registry <repo> [--test <id>] [--since <duration|date>] [--output text|json]` reads the run records in the registry and aggregates them on demand: runs, hit rate, misses by cause, verification failures, shadow false hits, and total and saved test time, per test and in total. It writes nothing. Records that are unreadable, oversized, malformed or inconsistent are skipped and counted; reading stops after 1000 records or 64 pages per listing, with a warning. New format, provisional: `aval.trianalab.io/stats-report/v1` (`pkg/evidence` adds `StatsReport`, `TestStats`, `RunStats`, `MissCauses` and `StatsReportVersion`). Run records gain the optional `verifiedResult`, and `evidence.ParseRunRecord` now rejects inconsistent records (`reused: true` requires outcome `verified` and `testDurationMs` 0; `verifiedResult` requires outcome `verified`), so `aval record` refuses a hand-edited record that claims a reuse that never happened. Docs explain pruning old records with ORAS.
